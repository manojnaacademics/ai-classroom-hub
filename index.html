Experiment #01 V0 vs 1/λ Linear Fit

Determine Planck's Constant ($h$)

Formula: $$h = \frac{e}{c} \times \frac{\Delta V_0}{\Delta(1/\lambda)} = \frac{e}{c} \times \text{Slope}$$ Standard constant: $h = 6.626 \times 10^{-34} \text{ J}\cdot\text{s}$.

🔴 Red LED ($\lambda_1 = 650 \text{ nm}$) 1.90 V
🟡 Yellow LED ($\lambda_2 = 585 \text{ nm}$) 2.12 V
🟢 Green LED ($\lambda_3 = 525 \text{ nm}$) 2.36 V
🔵 Blue LED ($\lambda_4 = 470 \text{ nm}$) 2.64 V
Calculated Slope ($\Delta V_0 / \Delta(1/\lambda)$): 1.24 × 10⁻⁶ V·m
Planck's Constant ($h_{exp}$): 6.618 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s
Experimental Error: 0.12%

Interactive Plot: Knee Voltage $V_0$ vs $1/\lambda$

Observation Rule: The knee voltage $V_0$ is directly proportional to frequency $\nu = c/\lambda$. When $e V_0 = h \nu$, the photon energy matches the energy required to overcome the junction barrier.
