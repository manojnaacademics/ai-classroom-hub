Experiment #01 V0 vs 1/λ Fit

Determine Planck's Constant ($h$)

Formula: $h = \frac{e}{c} \times \frac{\Delta V_0}{\Delta(1/\lambda)} = \frac{e}{c} \times \text{Slope}$
Standard theoretical value: $6.626 \times 10^{-34} \text{ J}\cdot\text{s}$.

🔴 Red LED ($\lambda_1 = 650 \text{ nm}$) 1.90 V
🟡 Yellow LED ($\lambda_2 = 585 \text{ nm}$) 2.12 V
🟢 Green LED ($\lambda_3 = 525 \text{ nm}$) 2.36 V
🔵 Blue LED ($\lambda_4 = 470 \text{ nm}$) 2.64 V
Experimental Slope: 1.24 × 10⁻⁶ V·m
Planck's Constant ($h_{exp}$): 6.618 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s
Experimental Deviation: 0.12%

Linear Fit: $V_0 \text{ vs. } 1/\lambda$

Physical Concept: As forward voltage increases, current starts flowing at knee voltage $V_0$. The threshold photon energy $h\nu$ matches the band barrier $e V_0$.
